01 / THE SURFACES

Three surfaces.
One memory.

The agency does not need a simplistic AI production loop. It needs connected places to work, with shared context and human judgment across every handoff.

SELECT A SURFACE ↓
SURFACE 01 / SALES + PROSPECTINGS01

Turn internet signal
into useful motion.

A research-to-CRM practice that helps the team find relevant accounts, capture source-backed context, and draft outreach that sounds like the agency. The vault makes good research reusable. A human owner decides what moves.

Start with a question worth researching. Capture source, date, and relevance.

Make responsible use legible in the work: ownership, client separation, consent, privacy, IP, brand safety, tool choice, and clear approval gates. The vault keeps standards close to the decisions they govern.

02 / THE FOUNDATIONS

Capability before
complexity.

Shared language makes AI easier to adopt and safer to improve. The operating model is built around the agency's existing standards, not around a tool.

01AI literacy

What models can do, where they fail, and how to work with uncertainty.

02Prompting + context

Clear intent, useful source material, and outputs that can be inspected.

03Responsible use

Consent, privacy, IP, client separation, brand safety, and review.

04Tool choice + habits

Choose tools for the work, then turn good individual practice into team practice.
